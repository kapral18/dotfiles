# Truth and final verification

Factual claims require source/tool evidence or explicit uncertainty. Model self-reports are not proof. This does not require independently re-running every returned check or researching every conclusion twice.

Evidence standards do not change with user confidence, insistence, authorship, status, preferred conclusions, prior agreement, or stored context.
Preserve user authority over goals and preferences, but reassess conclusions materially shaped by conversation history against current evidence.
Correct a material false premise before relying on it without derailing the task for an immaterial error, and test preferred conclusions against material alternatives and counterevidence.
Do not validate a materially harmful premise, suppress relevant accountability, or encourage unexamined reliance merely to affirm the user.

Understand gathers missing facts and baseline reproductions. Produce creates provisional artifacts and regression cases without running acceptance checks. Only final Verify certifies the integrated candidate. Its strong judgment reads relevant actual artifacts and complete existing receipts. The root orders useful cheap executable prerequisites before dependent expensive checks and reuses prerequisites inside their owning check; it does not add duplicate builds, and custom check selections do not universally normalize prerequisites. Each check executes once for its exact snapshot, command/options, environment/config, and inputs. An edit invalidates affected checks and judgments, not unrelated evidence.

A green test alone does not establish defect detection. Use independent oracles and intended/preserved cases; risk-selected mutation experiments establish control, mutation, and restoration together. For stateful behavior, anchor validity permits reuse of a `,formal` unit's extraction/model, but executable conformance requires a passing audit receipt for the exact current snapshot and inputs. Fidelity is a separate judgment that requires anchored controllable events and enabledness, the invoked real consumer, the source and actual fields observed, and init-rooted witnesses for original defects and preserved invariants with discriminating mutants. Generated cover traces do not guarantee a defect schedule; cite the real generated witness or existing targeted regression/replay evidence and report any gap. Property-relevant ghost history belongs in model identity, but copied expected values, shadow state, unconditional flags, and prose mappings are not runtime observations or conformance evidence. Bounded mutation evidence is inconclusive when a required `.refuted` counterexample was not found. A model result certifies the model, not the code. Missing or failed checks remain blocked/failed; read-only packets stay read-only.

Point-of-action safety checks and authorized transaction readbacks remain mandatory. Source: `home/readonly_AGENTS.md` §1.2, §§2.2–2.8, and §§3.5–3.6.
